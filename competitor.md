## 💰 1. Pricing Structure & Business Model Breakdown

### A. StayBind (`staybind.com`)
* **Pricing Model:** Per-Property Tiered Model + Per-Unit Add-ons + Feature Add-ons (**Prices are EXCLUSIVE of 18% GST**).
* **Plans:**
  * **Starter:** ₹999 / property / month (+ 18% GST = ₹1,179). **Hard cap of max 2 properties**. Includes OTA sync, basic calendar, templates. No automated journeys, no GST invoicing.
  * **Growth:** ₹1,999 / property / month (+ 18% GST = ₹2,359). Unlimited properties. Adds automated guest journeys, GST invoicing, team roles, analytics.
  * **Scale:** Custom quote for large portfolios.
* **Add-ons Stack:**
  * **Additional Units:** +₹199 / unit / month (+ GST) for units beyond the 1st unit per property.
  * **WhatsApp Business Automation:** +₹499 / property / month (+ GST).
  * **AI Guest Concierge:** +₹799 / property / month (+ GST).
  * **Custom Domain:** ₹999 / year.
  * **Onboarding & Migration:** ₹9,999 one-time.
* **Billing Frequency:** Monthly, cancel anytime.

### B. Doorloom (`doorloom.com`)
* **Pricing Model:** Stacking Modular Formula (`Account Fee + Property Base + Extra Units + Channel Manager`).
* **Base Account Fee:** ₹300 / month (mandatory).
* **Property Fee:** ₹300 / property / month (vacation rentals) or ₹1,000 / property (resorts >14 keys).
* **Extra Units:** ₹50 / unit / mo (rooms) or ₹100 / unit / mo (villas).
* **Channel Manager:** Paid add-on: ₹100 / unit / mo across *all* keys.
* **Billing Frequency:** **Quarterly (3 months upfront) or Yearly (10% off) ONLY**. No monthly option. (GST included).

### C. MyHomestay.ai (`myhomestay.ai`)
* **Pricing Model:** Opaque Custom Annual Subscription.
* **Market Quote:** ~₹18,000 – ₹30,000 / year (~₹1,500 – ₹2,500 / month equivalent).
* **Billing Frequency:** **100% Annual Upfront Only**.
* **Current Status:** ⚠️ **Onboarding is currently CLOSED**; Channel Manager is unreleased ("Launching soon").

### D. GroundCode Pro (`ground-code.com`)
* **Pricing Model:** Base Platform + Predictable Key Expansion.
* **Plan:** ₹1,499 / month (Includes property + 1st key + **100% features & 2-way Channel Manager included**).
* **Extra Keys:** +₹350 / key / month across the entire portfolio.
* **Billing Frequency:** **Month-to-month**. 30-Day Free Trial. No contracts, no quarterly lock-in.

## 💡 4. Detailed Evaluation of StayBind vs. GroundCode

### Why StayBind Becomes Extremely Expensive for Multi-Property Owners
1. **The "Per-Property" Multiplier**:  
   StayBind charges **₹1,999 for EVERY property** once you have more than 2 locations. With 3 properties (7 rooms, 2 rooms, 1 farmhouse), your base platform fee is already **₹5,997/mo** before adding a single room unit!
2. **The "Extra Unit" Surcharge**:  
   Each property in StayBind only covers 1 base unit. Your 7 extra rooms cost another ₹199/unit/mo (+₹1,393).
3. **Hidden GST & Add-on Tolls**:  
   StayBind's prices look reasonable until you realize:
   - GST (18%) is **extra** (adds ~₹1,330/mo).
   - WhatsApp automation is **not included** (costs an extra ₹499/property/mo = ₹1,497/mo).
   - Total StayBind cost shoots up to **₹8,720 to ₹10,486/month**.
   - By comparison, GroundCode delivers your entire 3-property, 10-key portfolio for **₹4,649/month** — almost **half the price of StayBind**!

### The Operational Blindspot
Like Doorloom and MyHomestay.ai, **StayBind has zero on-ground floor operations tools**:
- No Telegram bot for ground staff (staff must log into browser dashboards).
- No Kitchen Order Screen or meal confirmation system for cooks.
- No physical cash drawer reconciliation or photo-verified staff receipts for grocery and maintenance runs.
