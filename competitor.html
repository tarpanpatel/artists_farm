Ready to be tested for a wider audience — starting with smaller properties first

Full Hotel-Grade Subsystems

GroundCode is not a superficial booking calendar. Under the hood, we actually have nearly everything a complete property management system should have, even for a hotel:

  • • Multi-room reservation rack & live housekeeping states
  • • Split guest folios, kitchen KDS & automated meal billing
  • • Shift petty cash balancing with photo receipts & staff khata
  • • Staff daily attendance, advance tracking & automated payroll
  • • 2-way OTA channel sync (Channex), WhatsApp vouchers & digital guest ID capture

Ready to Be Tested for a Much Wider Audience: In other words, the core product architecture is functionally ready to be tested and deployed for much larger hospitality operations, including boutique hotels.

Why We Are Starting With Smaller Properties: Of course, it has not yet been tested at large enterprise scale.

Starting exclusively with smaller properties (homestays, private estates, and boutique resorts under 25 keys) is our intentional strategy. It ensures we directly supervise every live workflow, catch subtle ground edge cases, and guarantee 100% operational reliability before expanding outwards.
