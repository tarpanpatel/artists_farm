* **Universal 30-Day Free Trial**: Every new property owner receives a **1-Month (30 Days) Free Trial** upon registration. No credit card information is required, and there is no lock-in commitment.

## PART II: PRIVACY POLICY

### 1. Information We Collect
Ground Code collects information strictly required to operate homestay management services:
* **Property Owner Data**: Name, phone number (for OTP authentication), email address, property name/address, GSTIN, and FSSAI license details.
* **Guest Operational Data**: Guest names, contact numbers, check-in/out dates, ID proof uploads (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License) collected for statutory police verification registers.
* **Staff & Device Logs**: Staff names, assigned PIN hashes, shift clock-in/out timestamps, and IP addresses used to access the PMS dashboard.

---

### 2. How We Use Data & Non-Disclosure Guarantee
We use collected data strictly to:
1. Provide, maintain, and optimize the Ground Code PMS and KDS software.
2. Transmit staff notifications and guest receipts via designated messaging channels (Telegram / WhatsApp links).
3. Generate property financial summaries, petty cash logs, and inventory depletion reports for the Property Owner.
4. Assist property owners in maintaining statutory guest registers required by local authorities.

* **STRICT ZERO DATA SELLING GUARANTEE**: Ground Code **NEVER** sells, rents, or monetizes property data, guest databases, or financial records to third-party ad networks, data brokers, or competing properties.

---

### 3. Security & Data Protection
* **Encryption**: Data in transit is encrypted using standard TLS/HTTPS protocol.
* **Hashed PIN Credentials**: Staff PINs and passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing algorithms.
* **Restricted Access**: Guest ID document uploads are restricted to authorized property staff and administrators for statutory compliance verification.
