# GroundCode Brand Manifesto Rules

Strictly follow these rules across all marketing, landing pages, competitive comparisons, UI text, and customer communication:

## 1. Core Philosophy & Positioning
- **Philosophy 1:** First ever app covering every aspect of hospitality, most importantly operations, which is difficult and is the ground reality in India.
- **Philosophy 2:** Cash is King, but it is hard to save and track cash. Our app does that for you.
- **Philosophy 3:** Simplicity. Zero complex apps. Ground staff uses Telegram (cleaners, watchmen, room service, waiters) with zero app installations.
- **Target Audience:** Homestay, Vacation Rental & Guesthouse Hosts (1–18 rooms) and professional hosts managing up to 80 distributed properties, but **NOT** corporate hotel chains.
- **Core Driver:** Operations Ease & Daily Peace of Mind.

## 2. Copywriting & Content Rules
- **No line longer than 10 words.** Keep all lines, bullets, and sentences punchy, short, and scannable.
- **Maximum visuals, images, and icons — least words.**
- **Friendly Homestay Host Tone:** Write like talking to a friendly homestay host, never like an academic whitepaper or enterprise corporate presentation.
- **No corporate jargon:** Never lecture about "money leakage" or "pennywise profit". Small business owners care about operations ease; smooth operations naturally eliminate loss.
- **Never show fake URLs:** (e.g. `domain.com/path`) in screenshots or examples.
- **Never state the obvious:** (e.g., ❌ *"See Inside The App"*).
- **Never mention tablets:** Clients must feel their mobile phone is completely sufficient.
- **Product Scope Honesty:** Mention that GroundCode actually incorporates nearly everything a complete hotel PMS should have and is architecturally ready for wider audiences, but we deliberately start with smaller properties first for operational stability.

## 3. Communication Channel Protocol
- **Telegram is strictly for staff operations:** Zero new apps to learn or install. Cleaners, caretakers, guards, and cooks use Telegram tap buttons, voice notes, and photo uploads.
- **WhatsApp is strictly for guests:** Direct booking vouchers, checkout bills, folios, and fast quotes. Never mention WhatsApp for staff operations.
- **Web App for Everything:** No apps to install from any app store. Guests book directly without creating an account.

## 4. Messaging Cheat Sheet

### ❌ NEVER SAY:
- ❌ *"Stop money leakage & pennywise losses"*
- ❌ *"Run your stay on autopilot"*
- ❌ *"Zero sync lag"*
- ❌ *"AI revenue management & pricing engine"*
- ❌ *"Complex enterprise software for hotel chains"*
- ❌ WhatsApp for staff (WhatsApp is *only* for guest vouchers/bills).
- ❌ Tablets (Mobile phone is always enough).

### ✅ ALWAYS SAY:
- ✅ *"Effortless daily operations for your stay."*
- ✅ *"Staff uses only Telegram with zero new apps."*
- ✅ *"No stress of onboarding guards or cleaners."*
- ✅ *"Share booking vouchers & bills directly on WhatsApp."*
- ✅ *"Save 15–25% OTA fees on direct bookings."*
- ✅ *"Keep 100% room earnings on repeat guests."*
- ✅ *"What took hours now takes minutes."*
- ✅ *"Total clarity on your daily floor operations."*
